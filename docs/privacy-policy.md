# Anime Tracker — Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

## Data on your device

Anime Tracker stores your website configurations, approved addresses, series titles, episode URLs, cover image URLs, watch history, playback positions, and playback dates on your device. These records provide your watchlist, progress highlights, and optional resume. The app requires no Anime Tracker account and does not send these records to an Anime Tracker server. Device language preferences choose English or Italian.

## Websites and images

Opening a website connects to that website and its embedded services. Loading a cover image connects to the image host. These services receive network information such as your IP address and may use cookies, account details you enter, advertising, or analytics under their own policies. Review their privacy policies before using them. Anime Tracker does not control these services. Website login and browsing data can remain in the embedded browser.

## Exports and backups

Exports and backups are created only when you request them. A watchlist export contains series names, highest watched episodes, totals, and completion status. A full backup also contains website configurations, episode URLs, playback dates, and detailed progress. Sharing sends the file to the destination you choose, whose privacy policy applies. Device or cloud backups may also include app data, depending on your system settings.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or delete the app. Remove individual series or use Clear (all) in Watch List to erase watch history. Delete websites separately in Settings; deleting a website keeps watch history. To stop automatic playback recording, remove that website and use the manual watchlist. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local app data; manage device backups separately. Delete exported files and shared copies at their destinations. Anime Tracker cannot delete information held by websites; use their privacy controls for cookies, accounts, and deletion requests.

## Advertising and analytics

Anime Tracker includes no advertising or analytics service of its own and does not sell your watch history. Saving local playback progress is different from advertising tracking. Third-party websites may display ads or collect information under their own policies.

## Website access

Use websites and content you are authorized to access, and follow each service’s terms. Anime Tracker does not supply, host, or download videos and is not affiliated with the websites you configure. Compatibility depends on the website and player.
